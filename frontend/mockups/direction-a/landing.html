How it works

  1. Link your handle

    Enter your Codeforces handle. AlgoLearn checks it and imports your submissions.

  2. Read your report

    See strength per topic and per pair, like bitmasks+dp, with the numbers behind each score.

  3. Practise the gap

    Solve the next problems we pick, 100–300 above your comfort rating for that topic.

What you get

Hints before answers
When you are stuck, hints arrive one at a time. The full solution waits until you ask for it.
The concept you were missing
“This needs DP and combinatorics. Your combinatorics strength is 0.29.” Then three easier problems on it.
Similar problems, any input
Paste text, a link, a PDF or a screenshot. Matches come from about 11,700 Codeforces problems.
Never a blank page
If the AI is busy, you still get likely topics, your weak areas, similar problems and the editorial.

For practice and upsolving only

AlgoLearn is built for the hours between contests. Do not use it during a live rated contest or a placement test: that breaks their rules, and it would not teach you anything.
